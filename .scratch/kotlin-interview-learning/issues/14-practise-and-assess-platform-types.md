# 14: Practise and assess Platform types

**What to build:** Let a learner reason through the Platform types interview question, compare their thinking with an authored rubric, and explicitly assess their understanding without the app inferring progress from reading or revealing.

**Blocked by:** 11: Study Platform types in a Focused Lesson

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The focused interview question is hidden until the learner reaches its lesson section and its reasoning remains hidden until an explicit reveal action.
- [ ] Optional scratch space accepts an answer but never requires one before reveal.
- [ ] Scratch content lasts only for the current interaction and is not included in stored progress.
- [ ] The reveal action presents essential points, trade-offs, common traps, and likely follow-up probes rather than a memorization-sized answer.
- [ ] A new visit starts with the reasoning hidden even when the concept has an assessment.
- [ ] Reading the lesson, reaching its end, writing scratch text, or revealing reasoning does not change assessment state.
- [ ] The learner can explicitly choose Needs review, Can explain, or Interview-ready; absence of a choice remains Not assessed.
- [ ] Reassessment can promote or downgrade the concept and records the latest assessment date without preserving a history timeline.
- [ ] The assessment control explains that states are learner-owned judgments rather than scores or certification.
- [ ] Desktop acceptance tests prove that scratch and reveal are ephemeral and independent from explicit assessment.
